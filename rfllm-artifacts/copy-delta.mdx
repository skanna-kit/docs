### What is a credit? (live on the pricing page, right under the tier grid)

**What is a credit?**

One credit runs one tracked prompt against one standard AI model. Frontier models use 3 credits per run. Premium models (Grok) use 4.

Here's what that means in practice:

- Track 10 prompts daily across 4 standard models = **1,200 credits/month**
- Add 5 priority prompts daily on 2 frontier models = **+900 credits/month**
- Need a one-time push for a launch? **Buy a top-up, no subscription change.**

The pricing page includes a live calculator below this line so prospects can model their expected usage before signing up.

### Pricing FAQ

**Q: Why credits instead of "50 prompts, 4 models" like everyone else?**
Because some customers want 200 shallow prompts weekly, and some want 10 prompts hourly on frontier models. A flat count would force them into one shape. Credits let you spend your budget the way your category needs.

**Q: Will I hit my credit limit?**
You shouldn't, if you're tracking at a normal cadence. The dashboard shows your projected end-of-month balance so you'll know days in advance. If you do hit it, nothing breaks — scheduled runs pause, we email you, and you can top up starting at $19 or upgrade tier.

**Q: Do unused credits roll over?**
Subscription credits: no, they refresh every billing cycle. Top-up credits: yes, they're yours for 90 days after purchase.

**Q: What counts as a "standard" vs "frontier" model?**
Standard = the cheaper, faster API tier of each provider (GPT-4o-mini, Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Perplexity Sonar). Frontier = the full models that power the ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Perplexity Pro consumer apps (GPT-5, Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Sonar Pro). Standard is ~90% of the signal at a third of the cost; frontier is what to use when a prompt's exact wording matters.

**Q: Can I see what a prompt will cost before I save it?**
Yes. Every prompt creation screen shows "This prompt costs X credits per run. At daily runs, that's Y credits/month." You know before you commit.

**Q: What if I run out mid-month?**
Your tracking pauses, not your account. We email you; you either top up ($19 for 1,000 credits / $49 for 3,000 / $139 for 10,000 / $349 for 30,000 / $999 for 100,000) or upgrade. Nothing is billed without you clicking.

**Q: Do you use my prompts or data to train models?**
No. We send your prompts to standard API endpoints with training-disabled flags (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Perplexity all offer this by default on their paid API). Your prompts and the answers stay in your workspace.

**Q: What's Prompt Studio?**
A one-time scan of your site that generates 50 buyer prompts tailored to your category. $29 separately, free on Growth and Scale trials.

**Q: Do you have a free tier?**
No — but our `/audit/[domain]` URL and all 10 free tools let you try real ReadyForLLM data without signing up. The 14-day paid-plan trial is a full Starter with 6,000 trial credits plus Prompt Studio as a gift.

**Q: Team seats?**
1 on Starter, 5 on Growth, unlimited on Scale. Extra seats on Starter cost $19/mo each.

**Q: What about annual billing?**
15% off, roughly two months free. Cancel any time; unused months prorate refund. Credits still reset monthly — annual is just billing cadence, not credit allocation.

**Q: Nonprofit / OSS discount?**
50% off any self-serve plan for registered 501(c)(3)s and maintainers of OSS projects with 1k+ GitHub stars. Email hello@readyforllm.com.

## 4. Dashboard microcopy — credit meter copy (added to Part 8)

### The persistent header meter, three states

**State A — Healthy (balance `>` 20% of monthly allocation):**
`>` ⚡ {balance} / {allocation} credits · resets in {daysRemaining} days

**State B — Low (balance 5–20%):**
`>` ⚠️ {balance} / {allocation} credits · resets in {daysRemaining} days · [Top up]

**State C — Exhausted (balance `<` 5%):**
`>` 🔴 {balance} credits · {pausedPromptCount} prompts paused · [Top up] · [Upgrade to {nextTier}]

### The expanded side panel (on meter click)

*Panel header:* Credit usage

*Current balance section:*
`>` **{balance} credits** of {allocation} monthly allocation
`>` At your current rate (~{rate}/day), you'll use {projectedConsumption} before {resetDate}.
`> {advisory — see below}`

*Advisory copy, dynamic:*
- If projected `>` allocation: "You're trending {overagePct}% over — consider a {packSize}-credit top-up or upgrading to {nextTier}."
- If projected 70–99% of allocation: "You're on track to use most of this month's credits. Nice pacing."
- If projected `<`50%: "You're using well under your allocation. Consider tracking a few more prompts, or downgrading to {lowerTier} if this is typical."

*Recent consumption section header:* Top 5 prompts by credit spend this month

*Recent transactions section header:* Last 20 transactions

*CTAs at bottom of panel:*
- [Top up credits →]
- [Upgrade to {nextTier} →] (only shown if tier-upgrade math makes sense)
