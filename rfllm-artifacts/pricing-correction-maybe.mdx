**Date:** April 16, 2026

## Correction 1: Token math was too optimistic

### What was wrong

v4 assumed 100 input tokens per model query. Reality: we send a **system prompt** (~120 tokens: "You are a helpful AI assistant answering a buyer's question about a product category. Include specific company/product names. Format as 2–3 short paragraphs..."), plus **category/context hints** (~80 tokens), plus the customer's actual prompt text (up to 125 tokens).

**Realistic input: ~300 tokens per model query.** Plus extraction pass input goes from 1,000 → 1,200 tokens (longer real-world answers), and extraction output from 200 → 250 tokens.

### What it does to margins

| Model | Old cost/run | New cost/run | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| gpt-4o-mini | $0.00249 | $0.00297 | +19% |
| claude-haiku-4-5 | $0.00610 | $0.00675 | +11% |
| gemini-2.5-flash | $0.00403 | $0.00454 | +13% |
| perplexity-sonar | $0.00290 | $0.00355 | +22% |
| gpt-5 | $0.01013 | $0.01083 | +7% |
| claude-sonnet-4-5 | $0.01430 | $0.01535 | +7% |
| gemini-2.5-pro | $0.01013 | $0.01083 | +7% |
| perplexity-sonar-pro | $0.01430 | $0.01535 | +7% |
| grok-4 | $0.01430 | $0.01535 | +7% |

**Why the damage is small:** output tokens are the expensive part (10x more expensive than input on most models). Raising input 3x barely moves the needle on models where output dominates.

### Revised tier margins

| Tier | Price | Credits | Realistic COGS | Margin $ | Margin % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $89 | 6,000 | $29.40 | $59.60 | **67.0%** |
| Growth | $249 | 20,000 | $93.76 | $155.24 | **62.3%** |
| Scale | $799 | 80,000 | $363.48 | $435.52 | **54.5%** |

**Worst case (100% cheap model usage — cheapest per credit for customers, most expensive per credit for us):**

| Tier | Worst-case COGS | Worst-case Margin |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $31.17 | **65.0%** |
| Growth | $99.66 | **60.0%** |
| Scale | $387.06 | **51.6%** |

**Net impact:** margins drop ~1 percentage point across all tiers. Still healthy. Pricing stays at $89 / $249 / $799. No subscription price change needed.

## Correction 2: Trial abuse mitigation

### The concern

If someone starts a trial, burns all 6,000 credits, then leaves — we're out the LLM costs with nothing back. At 200 trials/month and 30% conversion, 140 non-converters could theoretically burn through ~$2,000/month in LLM waste.

### The reality before mitigation

A customer starting a 14-day Starter trial can burn **at most 6,000 credits**. Here's the realistic harm scenarios:

**Scenario A: Natural max-usage (well-intentioned but doesn't convert)**
- 50 prompts × 14 days × 4 cheap models = 2,800 credits naturally consumed
- Cost to us: 2,800 × $0.00408 = **$11.42**
- Almost harmless.

**Scenario B: Abuse — customer fills trial with prompts on day 1, lets it run**
- 6,000 credits consumed across 14 days
- Cost to us: **$24.48 per abused trial**
- Still not catastrophic per-case, but cumulative at scale.

**Scenario C: Viral moment (200 trial signups in a week)**
- Worst case if ALL bomb and leave: 200 × $24.48 = **$4,896**
- But realistically ~30–50% of signups truly bomb: ~$1,500–$2,500 in waste in one week

**Month-1 economics at 200 trials/mo, 30% conversion:**
- Trial costs: ~$3,231 in LLM COGS
- New MRR: $5,340 (60 new customers × $89)
- **Net month-1 profit: +$2,108**

So even unmitigated, trial math works **as long as conversion stays above ~20%.** Below 20% conversion, net profit flips negative.

### The mitigation combo: 3 changes, minor engineering, big downside protection

**Change 1: Trial is 7 days, not 14**
- Most competitive SaaS tools with real daily-run value use 7-day trials (Linear, Cal.com, Attio all do)
- Naturally caps credit burn to ~2,800 credits under normal usage
- Small conversion-rate risk (~5-10% fewer conversions per industry benchmark)
- **Engineering:** trivial — change the constant in `sites.trial_ends_at` grant

**Change 2: Trial runs only standard models, never frontier**
- Trial customer wants to see what a paid Starter looks like — Starter doesn't get frontier anyway, so this matches
- Prevents "try frontier models for free" exploit
- Makes the Growth upgrade path more concrete: "frontier models available on Growth"
- **Engineering:** trivial — check `isTrial` flag on prompt creation, restrict model set

**Change 3: Trial throttle of 500 credits/day**
- Forces distributed, authentic usage patterns
- Prevents "open trial, add 50 prompts, fire force-reruns, leave" bomb pattern
- Real customers don't notice (they add prompts over days, not minutes)
- Abusers hit the throttle instantly
- **Engineering:** add a daily-window check in `CreditService.consume()` — if trial site AND `consumed_today + amount > 500`, block run and message "Trial throttle reached for today — resumes midnight UTC"

### Max exposure after mitigation

- Max credits burn per trial: **7 days × 500/day = 3,500 credits** = **$14.28 loss per bombed trial**
- vs unmitigated $24.48 loss per bombed trial
- **42% reduction in per-trial exposure**

### Monthly savings at 200 trial volume

- OLD waste (140 non-converters × 60% burn of 6k): **$2,056/month**
- NEW waste (140 non-converters × 60% burn of 3.5k): **$1,200/month**
- Monthly savings: **~$857**
- Annual savings: **~$10,282**

That's enough to fund Apollo Pro + LinkedIn Sales Nav + 2 other SaaS tools for the year.

### Copy update needed

Add to the Welcome email in Copy-Kit-v2.1-delta:

> Your 7-day trial includes:
> - **3,500 credits** across the trial period (500/day throttle keeps things simple)
> - All 4 standard AI models (GPT-4o-mini, Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Perplexity Sonar)
> - Full Starter feature set: citation tracker, Slack alerts, 50 tracked prompts
>
> On day 7, your trial auto-converts to Starter ($89/mo). Cancel any time.

Remove any mention of "14 days" from marketing and dashboard copy.

## Correction 3: Amazing free tools — the full catalog

Free tools are your single best asset. They cost very little to run, they compound through SEO, they're shareable, and every one is a lead-gen pipe that drops users into signup flows.

### Cost reality check

At 200 uses/day per tool (realistic for a niche B2B tool in its first year), **15 new tools cost ~$1,524/month** in LLM COGS. Even at wildly viral 1,000 uses/day each, ~$7,600/month.

Break-even: if these tools collectively drive **~570 Starter conversions/year**, they pay for themselves. At 3% tool-to-trial conversion, that's ~19,000 total tool uses to break even. Trivial at any real scale.

**The 10 existing free tools stay as-is.** Below are 15 NEW tool ideas worth building, ranked by expected lead-gen value.

### Tier S — Must build (highest lead-gen potential)

#### 1. **AI Readiness Score** — `/tools/ai-readiness-score`

One-URL tool. Enter your domain, we fetch it + analyze, return a 0–100 "AI readiness" score with a punch list of specific fixes.

**What it measures:**
- Has llms.txt? (+10 pts)
- Has structured data / schema.org markup? (+15 pts)
- Robots.txt AI-aware? (+10 pts)
- Pricing page exists and is crawlable? (+10 pts)
- FAQ page exists? (+10 pts)
- Documentation site linked? (+10 pts)
- Load speed `<`2s? (+10 pts)
- Structured content (H1/H2/H3 hierarchy)? (+10 pts)

**Why it's powerful:**
- Works on ANY domain — low friction
- Score is immediately shareable ("We scored 72/100 on ai-readiness")
- Punch list is actionable (customers can DIY the easy fixes themselves, and see the ones that require ReadyForLLM)
- Zero LLM cost — pure HTML fetch + analysis
- Cost: **$0.005 per run** (just Firecrawl fetch)

**CTA:** "Track your score daily in ReadyForLLM — we'll alert you when it changes."

### Tier C — Skip unless specific reason

- **Any tool that requires paid API integrations** (SerpApi, ZoomInfo, etc.) — these have marginal costs that scale linearly with usage, not flat like LLMs. Avoid at the free-tier layer.
- **Interactive "AI Chat" style tools** — customer types a conversation, we respond. Too open-ended, high cost, low signal on who's using it.
- **Tools that scrape competitor sites without consent** — ethical landmine, PR risk.
