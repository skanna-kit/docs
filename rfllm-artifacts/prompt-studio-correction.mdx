# ReadyForLLM — Pricing Patch (Prompt Studio Correction)

**Date:** April 16, 2026
**Purpose:** Correct the Prompt Studio positioning in Pricing-Economics-v4.md. Everything else in v4 remains in force.

---

## What's changing

**Old positioning (v4, section 3):**
`>` "Prompt Studio ($29 one-time, 92% margin, free on Growth+ trials) solves the cold-start problem."

**New positioning:**
`>` "Prompt Studio ($29 one-time, 92% margin) is a paid product. Customers unlock one free scan after their first successful payment. Growth/Scale customers get one free scan per month as a retention reward. Never free for trial users."

---

## Why the change

Giving Prompt Studio away free on trials creates a one-way cost sink:
- If 200 trials start in a busy month and 70% take the free Prompt Studio = 140 uses × $0.76 = $106/month in LLM COGS
- With 35% trial-to-paid, only 49 of those 140 convert → 91 freeloaders cost us $69/mo with zero revenue
- Worse: Prompt Studio is our **highest-margin product** (92%) — giving it away to non-converters is the exact opposite of what we should do with it
- Founders got value for free; non-converters got the product's juiciest feature before paying a dollar

**The corrected structure preserves all the lead-gen value while protecting margins:**

### Free preview at `/tools/prompt-studio-preview`
- 10 AI-generated prompts for any domain, no login
- Publicly shareable, designed to drive trial signups
- Rate limited: 3 uses/day/IP via existing RATE_LIMIT KV
- Our cost: ~$0.10/generation
- Purpose: lead magnet

### Paid: $29 one-time (unchanged)
- Full 50 prompts, categorized by funnel stage and persona
- Available to anyone (logged-in paid subscribers, trial users, walk-ups)
- Our cost: ~$0.76/generation
- Margin: 92%

### Paid customer reward (NEW)
- **After first successful subscription payment**, customer unlocks one free Prompt Studio scan
- **Growth and Scale customers get one free scan per month** — generates a refreshed prompt set as their business evolves
- **Starter customers get one free after first payment; subsequent uses are $29 each**
- Free customer scans are an intentional retention investment (cost: $0.76/scan × customer count)

---

## Economic impact of the correction

Assuming 200 trials/month, 70% attach rate on the (old) free offering:

**Under the old "free on trials" model:**
- 140 Prompt Studio generations per month × $0.76 = **$106.40 in COGS**
- 49 converters "earned" the free scan retroactively — fine
- 91 non-converters got it for free — pure cost

**Under the new "paid customer reward" model:**
- ~49 converters each get 1 free scan after first payment = 49 × $0.76 = **$37.24 in COGS**
- Of the remaining trials: maybe 15% purchase Prompt Studio at $29 during their trial to accelerate their setup = 30 purchases × ($29 − $0.76 − $1.30 Polar fee) = **$807 in margin**
- Plus Growth/Scale retention refresh scans (estimate 10/mo at $0.76 COGS each) = $7.60 COGS, valued at ~$29 × 10 retention value
- **Net monthly improvement: ~$870 in revenue, ~$70 in COGS savings = ~$940/mo better**

**Annualized improvement at 200-trial-per-month volume: ~$11,300 of additional margin available for GTM reinvestment.**

That's enough to fund:
- Apollo Professional upgrade ($149/mo, unlocks 30K credits/mo vs 10K on Basic)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator for a second account (if you want to hire a sales contractor later)
- OR: 3 months of modest LinkedIn ads in year 2
