## 1. The credit unit — what it is and what it costs

### One credit = one "standard" AI model query

Specifically: one full prompt run against one cheap-tier model (GPT-4o-mini, Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Perplexity Sonar), including the extraction pass that parses brand and competitor mentions from the answer.

### Credit cost per model run

| Model class | Credits per run | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Cheap** | 1 credit | GPT-4o-mini, Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Perplexity Sonar, DeepSeek Chat, Llama 3.3 70B |
| **Frontier** | 3 credits | GPT-5, GPT-5 mini, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Perplexity Sonar Pro, Mistral Large |
| **Premium** | 4 credits | xAI Grok-4, experimental/newly-launched frontier models |
| **Drift summary** | +1 credit | Only triggers on ~20% of runs (when answer hash materially changed) |
| **Competitor extraction** | 0 credits | Already baked into the model run; no second call |

### Our real cost per credit (COGS)

- Cheap call: $0.0045 per credit = 1 credit/run ÷ $0.0045 = $0.0045/credit COGS
- Frontier call: $0.011 per run ÷ 3 credits = **$0.00367** /credit COGS
- Premium call: $0.015 per run ÷ 4 credits = $0.00375 /credit COGS

**Key insight:** Frontier and premium models cost us *less per credit* than cheap models, because we priced the credit-multiplier (3x, 4x) slightly above the raw cost ratio (2.5x, 3.3x). This means customers who use frontier/premium models are **more profitable per credit**, not less. Worst-case pricing works.

**Blended realistic COGS per credit** (70/25/5 mix): **$0.00425/credit**

### Our retail rate per credit (what customers pay)

- Starter: $0.01483/credit — headline impulse-buy tier
- Growth: $0.01245/credit — 16% bulk discount vs Starter
- Scale: $0.00999/credit — 33% bulk discount vs Starter
- Top-up packs: range from $0.019 (1k pack) to $0.00999 (100k pack)

**Gross markup vs COGS (Starter rate): 248%.** That's 3.5x our blended cost. Standard healthy SaaS gross markup is 4–6x; we're deliberately on the lower end to keep the entry price at $89 instead of pushing $149. Acceptable trade.

**Design rules:**

- Price per credit **decreases as pack size grows**, creating natural upgrade incentive toward bulk or toward upgrading tier.

## 4. The credit economy — why customers benefit, not just us

The credit system isn't just a pricing lever. It's **better product design** than flat "N prompts × M models" tier caps:

1. **Customer autonomy.** A Growth customer can run 200 prompts on cheap models or 50 prompts deeply across cheap+frontier. Same price. Their call. Tiers with flat model bundles force everyone into one shape of usage.

2. **Transparent economics.** The customer sees, in-dashboard, "This prompt costs 12 credits per run (4 cheap models daily = 120 credits/month)." They know what they're spending. No surprise invoices.

3. **Frontier model access is democratized, not gated.** Even a Starter customer can occasionally fire a frontier-model run on a critical prompt — they'll just need a top-up or they'll burn through faster. They have the *choice*.

4. **Cost predictability for us.** Every tier has a hard upper bound on LLM spend per customer per month. A runaway customer is structurally impossible.

5. **Upgrade path is mathematically motivated.** "You're using 18,500 of 6,000 credits this month via top-ups. Growth includes 20,000 at a better per-credit rate." Self-evident upsell.

## 5. The 14-day trial with Prompt Studio gift

**Trial flow (unchanged from v3):**
1. Prospect lands on `/audit/[domain]` — free, shareable, no signup.
2. CTA: "Track this daily — start a 14-day free trial." Credit card required.
3. Trial gives full Starter (6,000 credits, 4 cheap models, 50 prompts, daily, Slack alerts).
4. **Day 0 onboarding nudge:** "Want us to scan your site and propose 50 buyer prompts to track? Normally $29, free as part of your trial." Expected attach: 70%+.
5. Daily Sentinel runs fill the dashboard from day 1.
6. Day 12: pre-charge email with usage summary.
7. Day 14: auto-convert to Starter subscription.

**Target trial-to-paid conversion: 35%.** (Benchmark for CC-required B2B SaaS trials with strong day-1 value delivery: 25–40%.)

### Why no self-serve free tier

The audit URL and free tools do the "prove value without signup" job better than a free tier does. A free tier that grants 6,000 credits/mo would structurally bleed us at ~$25/customer/mo — and free-tier users convert at 2–5%. The trial model puts commitment in front of value delivery, which the target buyer (B2B SaaS with budget authority) expects and respects.

## 6. Unit economics sensitivity analysis

**Q: What if customers use 100% frontier/premium models?**
A: Covered. Worst-case margins are Starter 69.5%, Growth 65.4%, Scale 58.3%. All profitable.

**Q: What if our cache hit rate is lower than 30%?**
A: The credit pricing has cache-hit baked into COGS. If real-world caching delivers only 15% hits, per-credit COGS rises from $0.00425 to ~$0.0051. Starter margin drops to ~60% (from 66%). Still healthy. **Kill signal: if realized cache hit rate `<`20% for 3 consecutive months, revisit per-credit pricing.**

**Q: What if an LLM provider raises prices?**
A: We'd absorb up to a 25% cost increase without price changes (Starter margin still 55%+). Beyond that, adjust credit multipliers (e.g., frontier becomes 4 credits instead of 3). Customers see a transparent change; subscription price stays stable.

**Q: What if customers top up heavily instead of upgrading tier?**
A: Still profitable — every top-up pack is 52%+ margin. The in-app nudge at 2+ top-ups/mo pushes them toward tier upgrade, but we win either way.

**Q: What if customers under-utilize and feel they're overpaying?**
A: Real risk. If average Starter utilization ``<``40% after 90 days, consider either (a) dropping Starter to $69 with 4,500 credits, or (b) a $49 "Lite" tier with 3,000 credits for true hobbyists. Don't proactively change — let retention data tell the story.

## 8. Kill signals (revised for credit system)

Monitor monthly. Any trigger = diagnostic conversation, not automatic action.

| Metric | Tripwire | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Trial-to-paid conversion | `<`20% for 3 months | Trial isn't delivering value in 14 days. Shorten to 7 or add onboarding |
| Avg Starter credit utilization | `<`40% | Tier is oversized. Consider $69/4,500 Lite tier |
| Avg Starter credit utilization | >95% | Tier is undersized or customers are hitting limits. Raise allocation or nudge upgrade harder |
| Top-up frequency per customer | >2/mo average | Upgrade nudge isn't converting. Aggressive in-app banner |
| Frontier model usage (Growth+) | `<`10% of credits | Frontier differentiation isn't landing. Simplify to cheap-only, kill premium |
| Grok usage (Scale) | `<`5% of credits | Premium tier not valued. Kill Grok integration, reduce Scale to 9 models |
| Cache hit rate (our internal) | `<`20% for 3 months | Per-credit COGS rising. Tune cache strategy or raise credit multipliers |
| Churn month 4+ | >6% monthly | Product isn't sticky. Audit retention drivers |
| Prompt Studio attach on trials | `<`50% | Free offer isn't prominent enough in onboarding |
