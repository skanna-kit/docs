### Outbound prep

- [ ] Build ICP list: 500 B2B SaaS companies across dev tools, data, marketing, security verticals (use BuiltWith / Wappalyzer / LinkedIn).
- [ ] Run `/public/visibility-audit` on all 500 domains overnight.
- [ ] Manually review top 50 where audit surfaces something interesting.
- [ ] Generate `/audit/[domain]` URLs for those 50.

### Week 1 of launch (Monday of week 13)

- [ ] Post to HN: "Show HN: ReadyForLLM — AI visibility for B2B SaaS."
- [ ] X thread of pivot announcement.
- [ ] LinkedIn post of pivot announcement.

### Weeks 2–4 of launch

- [ ] 20 cold emails/day continued.
- [ ] Two blog posts per week.
- [ ] Three X threads per week, one LinkedIn post per week.
- [ ] Reply to every DM, mention, and HN comment within 4 hours.
- [ ] Ship one new free tool per week.

### Exit criteria for Phase 5

- 10+ paying customers (any mix of tiers).
- Audit URL has been run on 1,000+ domains organically.
- First customer conversation recorded and reviewed.
- At least one agency conversation initiated (for Phase 6).
